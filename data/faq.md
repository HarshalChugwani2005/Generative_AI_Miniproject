# ShopEasy Customer Support Knowledge Base

ShopEasy is an online shopping platform in India selling electronics, fashion, home and kitchen products, books and more. All prices are in Indian Rupees (₹). This knowledge base is used by the ShopEasy support assistant.

## Orders

### Q: How do I place an order on ShopEasy?
A: Search or browse for a product, select the size/colour if needed and tap "Add to Cart". Open your cart, tap "Proceed to Checkout", choose or add a delivery address, select a payment method and tap "Place Order". You will receive an order confirmation by SMS and email with your order ID (format: SE followed by 5 digits, for example SE12345).

### Q: Where can I find my order ID?
A: Your order ID starts with "SE" followed by 5 digits (for example SE12345). You can find it in the order confirmation email or SMS, or in the ShopEasy app/website under "My Account" > "My Orders".

### Q: Can I change my delivery address or items after placing an order?
A: You can change the delivery address only while the order status is "Placed" or "Processing", from "My Orders" > select the order > "Change Address". Items cannot be added to or removed from an order once it is placed. To change items, cancel the order (if it has not shipped) and place a new one.

### Q: Can I place an order without creating an account?
A: No. A ShopEasy account is required so that we can send order updates and process returns and refunds. Signing up is free and takes less than a minute using your mobile number or email address.

### Q: What should I do if I received a damaged, defective or wrong item?
A: Please report it within 48 hours of delivery from "My Orders" > select the order > "Report an Issue". Upload clear photos of the product and packaging. We will arrange a free replacement or a full refund after verification, usually within 2 business days.

### Q: What if an item is missing from my package?
A: Some orders are shipped in multiple packages, so please check "My Orders" to see if the remaining items are still in transit. If all packages are marked delivered and an item is missing, report it within 48 hours from "My Orders" > "Report an Issue" and our team will investigate within 2 business days.

## Order Tracking

### Q: How can I track my order?
A: Go to "My Account" > "My Orders" and select the order to see live tracking. You can also share your order ID (for example SE12345) with the support assistant in this chat to get the current status. A tracking link from our courier partner is also sent by SMS and email once the order is shipped.

### Q: What do the different order statuses mean?
A: "Placed" means we have received your order. "Processing" means the seller is packing it. "Shipped" means it has been handed to the courier. "Out for Delivery" means it will arrive today. "Delivered" means it has been delivered. "Cancelled" means the order was cancelled. "Return Requested" and "Refunded" relate to returns.

### Q: My order shows delivered but I have not received it. What should I do?
A: Please check with family members, neighbours or your building security, as couriers sometimes hand packages to them. If you still cannot find it within 24 hours, report it from "My Orders" > "Report an Issue" > "Not Received". We will investigate with the courier and resolve it within 3 business days with a re-delivery or full refund.

### Q: My order is delayed beyond the expected delivery date. What can I do?
A: Delays can happen due to weather, festivals or high order volumes. If your order is more than 2 days past the expected delivery date, please contact us with your order ID and we will escalate it with the courier partner. For delays of more than 7 days you may cancel the order for a full refund.

## Shipping

### Q: How long does delivery take?
A: Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days in metro cities and 5-8 business days in other cities and towns. Express delivery (available in selected metro cities) takes 1-2 business days. The exact expected delivery date is shown at checkout and in "My Orders".

### Q: What are the shipping charges?
A: Standard delivery is FREE for orders of ₹499 or more. For orders below ₹499, a shipping charge of ₹49 applies. Express delivery costs ₹99 extra per order. ShopEasy Plus members get free standard and express delivery on all orders.

### Q: Do you deliver to my location? Do you ship internationally?
A: ShopEasy delivers to over 19,000 PIN codes across India. Enter your PIN code on any product page to check delivery availability and the expected date. We do not ship internationally at this time.

### Q: Is same-day delivery available?
A: No, ShopEasy does not currently offer same-day delivery. The fastest option is Express delivery (1-2 business days), available in selected metro cities for ₹99 extra.

## Returns

### Q: What is the return policy?
A: Most products can be returned within 10 days of delivery. Electronics and appliances have a 7-day replacement-only window for defects. Items must be unused, with original tags, packaging and accessories. Non-returnable items include innerwear, cosmetics once opened, perishable goods, gift cards and personalised products.

### Q: How do I return a product?
A: Go to "My Orders", select the item and tap "Return". Choose a reason, upload photos if asked, and pick a pickup slot. Our courier will collect the item from your address free of charge. Please keep the product in its original packaging with all tags and accessories.

### Q: Are there any charges for returning a product?
A: No. Return pickup is free for all eligible returns. If a return is rejected after quality check (for example, the item is used or damaged by the customer), the product is sent back to you and no refund is issued.

### Q: Can I exchange a product for a different size or colour?
A: Yes. Fashion and footwear items can be exchanged for a different size or colour within 10 days of delivery, subject to stock availability. Go to "My Orders" > select the item > "Exchange". The new item is delivered when the old item is picked up.

## Refunds

### Q: How long does a refund take?
A: Once a return is picked up and passes quality check (usually within 2 days), the refund is initiated. UPI and ShopEasy Wallet refunds arrive within 1-2 business days. Credit/debit card and net banking refunds take 5-7 business days. Refunds for Cash on Delivery orders go to your ShopEasy Wallet or bank account within 3-5 business days.

### Q: How will I receive my refund for a Cash on Delivery order?
A: For Cash on Delivery (COD) orders you can choose to receive the refund either in your ShopEasy Wallet (within 1 business day) or by bank transfer (NEFT/IMPS) to your bank account (within 3-5 business days). You will need to add your bank account details when you request the return.

### Q: My refund has not arrived yet. What should I do?
A: First check the refund status in "My Orders" > select the order > "Refund Details", which shows the refund reference number (RRN). Banks may take up to 7 business days to credit card refunds. If it has been longer than the stated time, share the RRN with your bank, or contact ShopEasy support for escalation.

## Cancellations

### Q: How do I cancel an order?
A: You can cancel an order before it is shipped from "My Orders" > select the order > "Cancel Order" and choose a reason. Once the status is "Shipped" or "Out for Delivery", the order cannot be cancelled, but you can refuse the delivery or return it after delivery.

### Q: Is there a cancellation fee?
A: No. ShopEasy does not charge any fee for cancelling an order before it is shipped. If you prepaid, the full amount is refunded to your original payment method as per the refund timelines.

### Q: Can ShopEasy cancel my order?
A: In rare cases ShopEasy may cancel an order if the item is out of stock, the delivery address is not serviceable, the payment fails verification, or a pricing error is detected. You will be notified by SMS and email, and any amount paid will be refunded in full.

## Payments

### Q: What payment methods does ShopEasy accept?
A: ShopEasy accepts UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM and other UPI apps), credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay, American Express), net banking from all major Indian banks, ShopEasy Wallet, EMI, and Cash on Delivery (COD).

### Q: How does UPI payment work?
A: At checkout choose "UPI", then either enter your UPI ID (for example name@okbank) or select your UPI app. Approve the payment request in your UPI app within 5 minutes. If the money is debited but the order is not confirmed, it is automatically refunded within 3-5 business days.

### Q: Is Cash on Delivery (COD) available?
A: Yes, COD is available on orders between ₹100 and ₹50,000 for most PIN codes. A COD handling fee of ₹20 applies per order. You can pay the delivery agent in cash or by scanning their UPI QR code. COD may not be available for some high-value items.

### Q: Do you offer EMI options?
A: Yes. EMI is available on orders above ₹3,000 using credit cards from major banks (3, 6, 9, 12 and 18 month plans) and on selected debit cards. No-cost EMI is available on selected products, where the interest is given as an upfront discount. EMI interest rates are set by your bank.

### Q: Money was deducted but my order was not placed. What should I do?
A: Don't worry. If the payment was deducted but the order was not confirmed, the amount is automatically refunded to your original payment method within 3-5 business days. If you do not receive it within 7 business days, contact support with your transaction reference number.

### Q: Is it safe to save my card on ShopEasy?
A: Yes. ShopEasy follows RBI card tokenisation rules, so your actual card number is never stored. Payments are protected with PCI-DSS compliant systems and 3D Secure/OTP verification.

## Account and Login

### Q: I forgot my password. How do I reset it?
A: On the login screen tap "Forgot Password", enter your registered mobile number or email address, and enter the OTP you receive. You can then set a new password. You can also log in directly with an OTP without a password.

### Q: I am not receiving the OTP. What should I do?
A: Check that your mobile number or email is entered correctly and that you have network coverage. Wait 60 seconds before tapping "Resend OTP". Check your spam folder for email OTPs. If you have DND enabled, OTP SMS are still delivered. After 5 failed attempts, OTP requests are blocked for 30 minutes for security.

### Q: My account is locked or I think it was hacked. What should I do?
A: Accounts are temporarily locked for 30 minutes after 5 wrong password attempts. If you suspect unauthorised access, reset your password immediately and contact ShopEasy support at security@shopeasy.in or call 1800-123-4567 so we can secure your account and review recent orders.

### Q: How do I update my phone number, email or address?
A: Go to "My Account" > "Profile" to update your name, email or phone number (verified via OTP). Saved addresses can be added, edited or deleted under "My Account" > "Addresses".

### Q: How do I delete my ShopEasy account?
A: Go to "My Account" > "Settings" > "Delete Account". You must have no active orders, pending returns or pending refunds. Deletion is permanent and your ShopEasy Wallet balance, coupons and order history will be lost.

## Coupons and Offers

### Q: How do I apply a coupon code?
A: On the checkout page tap "Apply Coupon", enter the code or choose one from the list of available coupons, and tap "Apply". The discount is shown in the price summary before you pay. Only one coupon can be used per order.

### Q: Why is my coupon not working?
A: A coupon may not work if it has expired, the minimum order value is not met, it is limited to certain products, categories, payment methods or new users, or it has already been used. Check the coupon's terms on the "Apply Coupon" screen. Coupons cannot be applied after an order is placed.

### Q: What is the ShopEasy welcome offer?
A: New users get 10% off (up to ₹200) on their first order with the code WELCOME10, valid on a minimum order value of ₹999. The code can be used once per account.

### Q: What happens to my coupon if I cancel or return an order?
A: If you cancel or return a full order, the coupon is restored to your account if it is still within its validity period. For partial returns, the coupon discount is adjusted proportionally from the refund amount.

## Warranty

### Q: Do products come with a warranty?
A: Most electronics and appliances come with the manufacturer's warranty, usually 1 year, as stated on the product page. The warranty is provided by the brand, not by ShopEasy. Keep your invoice, which you can download from "My Orders" > "Download Invoice".

### Q: How do I claim a warranty?
A: Contact the brand's authorised service centre with your product and the ShopEasy invoice. Service centre details are on the product page and in the warranty card. ShopEasy support can help you find the service centre and escalate if the brand does not respond within 7 days.

### Q: What is ShopEasy Extended Protection?
A: ShopEasy Extended Protection is an optional plan you can buy with eligible electronics. It adds 1 or 2 extra years of coverage after the manufacturer's warranty ends and covers manufacturing defects (not physical or liquid damage). It must be purchased with the product or within 7 days of delivery.

## Contact and Escalation

### Q: How can I contact ShopEasy customer support?
A: You can chat with our support assistant here 24/7. Human support agents are available by phone at 1800-123-4567 (toll-free) from 9 AM to 9 PM IST, every day, and by email at support@shopeasy.in (replies within 24 hours).

### Q: How do I talk to a human agent or escalate a complaint?
A: In this chat, ask to speak to a human agent or click "Escalate to human agent". A support ticket will be created and a human agent will contact you within 24 hours. If your issue is still unresolved after 48 hours, you can email our grievance officer at grievance@shopeasy.in with your ticket ID.

### Q: What is a support ticket ID?
A: When an issue is escalated, a support ticket ID (format: TKT-YYYYMMDD-XXXXXX) is created. Please keep this ID and mention it whenever you contact ShopEasy so the agent can see your full issue history.

### Q: How do I become a seller on ShopEasy?
A: Visit sell.shopeasy.in and register with your GSTIN, PAN and bank account details. Seller support is available at sellers@shopeasy.in.
